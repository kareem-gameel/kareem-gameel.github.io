QM9 wB97M-V/def2-SVPD Dataset: 130,807 Molecular Energies

QM9 total electronic energies recomputed with Psi4 at ωB97M-V/def2-SVPD: the same functional and basis set as tmQM_wB97MV, for transfer tests from transition-metal complexes to small organic molecules.

Download on Zenodo ↗

What is included?

The CSV contains 130,807 unique original QM9 molecule identifiers and one absolute total electronic energy per molecule, expressed in both Hartree and kcal/mol. The two energy columns are unit conversions of the same quantity, not separate calculations.

Download the CSV or read the archived documentation. Zenodo is the authoritative, citable archive; this page describes the release without mirroring its data.

Relationship to QM9 and tmQM

These energies were recomputed from QM9 molecules; they are not the original QM9 energy labels. The calculation uses the ωB97M-V functional (also written wB97M-V or omegaB97M-V) and def2-SVPD basis set.

This matches the functional and basis set of tmQM_wB97MV, not original tmQM. A shared functional and basis set does not by itself establish identical numerical settings or energy referencing across datasets.

The energies supported transfer evaluation in the delta-learning study. Its common cross-baseline evaluation used 128,988 molecules, a subset of the 130,807 released here.

CSV columns and units

mol_id
Original QM9 molecule identifier. Join to source structures by this identifier, not row order.
total_electronic_energy_Ha
Absolute Psi4 total electronic energy in Hartree, preserving the cleaned export's value as written.
total_electronic_energy_kcal_per_mol
The Hartree value multiplied by 627.5094740631 and rounded to nine decimal places.

Scope and limitations

This release is an energy table, not a new geometry collection, force dataset, or set of thermal free energies. Refer to original QM9 for source structures and provenance. The release notes confirm the method, units, and identifier checks, but do not specify all numerical calculation settings, such as integration grids and convergence thresholds.

For cross-dataset comparisons, check geometry provenance, software conventions, and energy referencing rather than assuming that matching the functional and basis set guarantees interchangeable labels.
