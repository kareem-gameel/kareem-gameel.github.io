Privacy and Analytics

This website uses GoatCounter to understand which research pages people visit and which sites refer them. The analytics dashboard is private.

Analytics does not use cookies or persistent visitor identifiers. Page paths, page titles, referring domains, country-level locations, and basic browser and screen statistics are processed by GoatCounter. This integration omits URL query strings, fragments, and referring-page paths. See the GoatCounter privacy policy for its data-handling details.

Your Browser Preference

Analytics is not loaded on this page. JavaScript is needed to save an exclusion preference.

The preference is stored only in this browser. Enable it separately on each browser or device you use. Clearing site data removes it; private-browsing sessions do not inherit it.

Excluded browsers do not load the analytics script. Local previews and this privacy page are never counted.
