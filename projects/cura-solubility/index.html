06 · Molecular solubility

Graph-Based Solubility Prediction for Low-Carbon Cement

Predicting aqueous solubility to prioritize organic molecules for experimental evaluation in CURA's electrochemical cement process.

  • Aqueous Solubility
  • Molecular GNNs
  • Neuron-Sparse Learning
  • Low-Carbon Cement

Industrial context

Our partnership with CURA supports low-carbon cement production. CURA is developing an electrochemical process that uses electricity to generate acid and base, convert limestone into cement feedstock, and separate CO2 for capture. CURA's technology overview describes the process.

Molecular screening

Our research combines molecular simulations and machine learning to identify organic molecules that could improve process efficiency, including quinones and phenazines. My contribution focuses on predicting aqueous solubility to help prioritize candidates for experimental evaluation.

Graph-based prediction and transferability

I am developing graph-based machine-learning models for aqueous solubility and investigating neuron-sparse learning as a route to improved transferability across molecular structures. The goal is reliable screening of new candidates, with an emphasis on generalization beyond the training data.
